Ask the CSO (fictional office-coffee market in Riyadh; SAR).

**(a) Bottom-up market size**
- Segments:
  - small offices: 9,000 offices × 8 employees each
  - medium offices: 1,400 × 45
  - large offices: 220 × 300
- 60% of employees drink coffee, 16 cups per month each, at SAR 10 per cup.
- Compute the annual TAM by segment and in total.
- SAM is 35% of TAM (the share within our delivery radius). SOM is 4% of SAM.

**(b) Market concentration**
- Market shares (%): 32, 24, 14, 10 and 8, with the remaining 12% spread across 12 small firms of 1% each.
- Compute the HHI and the concentration band.

**(c) Entry options, scored 1–10**

| Option | value | risk (10 = lowest risk) | capital need (10 = least) | speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic | 6 | 7 | 8 | 4 |
| Partnership with a co-working chain | 7 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| Acquire a small competitor | 9 | 4 | 3 | 7 |

- Weights: value 0.35, risk 0.25, capital 0.20, speed 0.20.
- Which option wins, and is the ranking robust to ±20% changes in each weight?

Give your strategic recommendation.
