Ask the CFO to stress-test Project A, using exactly these Project A assumptions (fictional; SAR).

**Project A assumptions**
- Capex 2,400,000 at t=0. Life 6 years. Straight-line depreciation over 6 years.
- Pre-tax salvage 200,000, taxed at 20%.
- Year-1 volume 40,000 units, +5% a year. Year-1 price 95, +2% a year.
- Variable cost 58% of revenue. Fixed cash costs 650,000, +3% a year.
- NWC balance 12% of each year's revenue, recovered at the end.
- Tax 20% (losses offset other profits). Discount rate 11%.

**Required analysis**
1. A tornado of NPV for ±10% changes in each of: year-1 volume, year-1 price, variable-cost %, fixed cost and capex.
2. A reverse stress test: the break-even year-1 price, and the break-even year-1 volume (NPV = 0), each with its headroom versus the base case.
3. A combined downside: volume −25%, price −8%, variable cost 62% and fixed cost +10%, all applied together.
4. A Monte Carlo with 20,000 simulations:
   - year-1 price triangular (low 85, mode 95, high 100)
   - year-1 volume normal (mean 40,000, sd 6,000)
   - variable-cost % triangular (low 0.55, mode 0.58, high 0.64)
   - all independent
   Report the mean NPV, P(NPV < 0) and the 5th percentile.

Interpret the results for the CEO.
