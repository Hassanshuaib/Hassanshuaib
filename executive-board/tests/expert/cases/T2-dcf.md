Ask the CFO for a DCF valuation (fictional company "Najd Logistics"; SAR).

**Operating assumptions**
- Base-year (Y0) revenue 50,000,000. Revenue growth Y1–Y5: 12%, 10%, 8%, 6%, 5%.
- EBIT margin 18% every year. Tax 20%.
- D&A 4% of revenue; capex 6% of revenue.
- Net working capital = 15% of the same year's revenue (Y0 NWC = 15% of Y0 revenue).

**Cost of capital**
- rf 5.0%
- Peer levered beta 1.10 at peer D/E 0.30 (tax 20%). Unlever it, then relever at the company's target D/E of 0.25 (tax 20%).
- MRP 5.5%, plus a 1.0% country risk premium added to the cost of equity.
- Pre-tax cost of debt 7.0%.
- Target weights follow from D/E = 0.25.

**Terminal value and other inputs**
- Gordon growth 3%, and separately an exit multiple of 9.0x Y5 EBITDA.
- End-of-year discounting.
- Net debt 20,000,000; shares 10,000,000.

**Report**
- Cost of equity and WACC
- FCFF for Y1–Y5
- Enterprise value and value per share under both terminal methods
- Terminal value as a share of EV
- The implied exit multiple of the Gordon case, and the implied growth of the exit case
- A sensitivity of value per share to WACC (±1%) × g (2%, 3%, 4%)
- A reconciliation of the two methods
