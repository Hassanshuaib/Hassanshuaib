Ask the CFO for an investment feasibility analysis (fictional; SAR). Discount rate 11%; tax 20%. Assume losses can offset the company's other taxable profits.

**Project A (new production line)**
- Capex 2,400,000 at t=0. Life 6 years. Straight-line depreciation over 6 years to zero.
- Pre-tax salvage of 200,000 at the end of year 6, taxed at 20% (book value is zero).
- Year-1 volume 40,000 units, growing 5% a year. Year-1 price 95, growing 2% a year.
- Variable cost 58% of revenue. Fixed cash costs 650,000 in year 1, growing 3% a year.
- Net working capital: the balance equals 12% of each year's revenue. The change is a cash flow in that year, and the full balance is recovered at the end of year 6.

**Project B (smaller line, mutually exclusive with A)**
- After-tax cash flows: t0 −1,100,000; t1 380,000; t2 400,000; t3 420,000; t4 380,000.

Both projects could be repeated on the same terms at the end of their lives.

**Report for both projects**
- The yearly cash flows of A
- NPV, IRR, MIRR (finance and reinvestment rate 11%), payback, discounted payback, PI and the equivalent annual annuity

Then recommend which project to choose and why. Explain the economic reasoning behind the choice.
