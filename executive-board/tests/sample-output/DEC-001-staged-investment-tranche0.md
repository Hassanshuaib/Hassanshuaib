## 6. Financial implications (CFO)
Base NPV is about +88k (20%, owner salary 10k from Y2); downside about −575k. NPV is zero at an owner salary of ~15–16k/month. The base case turns negative at 1.5 offices/month, 12 seats, SAR 215, 5% churn, or 30-day invoicing. Most of the value comes from the owner's unpaid labour.

## 7. Operational implications (COO)
The CEO hits ~50 h/week at ~14 offices. A hiring ladder is required: a driver-helper at ~10 offices, and a first prep hire who can drive. Use our own recipe via a licensed producer, with a shelf-life test. Resilience items (SOPs/HACCP, a portal delegate, a backup producer) cost ~15k.

## 8. Other implications (market, strategy)
CMO: whole-office per-seat pricing works out at café price per cup drunk; test volume tiers of SAR 10–12.5/cup. Cold-only is a risk. CSO: location matters more than the kitchen; a residential base caps the reachable market; no moat today.
