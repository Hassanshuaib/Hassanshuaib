- **Tier:** Standard (5 independent analyses + 1 anonymised peer-review round by 2 reviewers).
- **Consulted:** CFO, COO, CMO, CSO, CRO. Peer reviewers: CRO and CFO.
- **Chair:** Chief of Staff.

- **CEO input:** runs the business personally; no salary in year 1.

## Verdict (Proposed; pending CEO confirmation, see DEC-001)
Do not commit SAR 300k to Option B as framed (own kitchen in a residential district). Approve only Tranche 0: about SAR 35k, 8–10 weeks, no lease, no hires. It must finish before Ramadan (~7 Feb 2027). Hold the remaining ~265k. Asset-light co-packing is the default. An own kitchen is considered only at ~25 offices, in a commercially zoned unit near an office corridor.

## Peer review
- **Strongest response:** both reviewers chose B (CRO). It showed the reserve gate is largely illusory, because Tranche 1 has no working capital: about 67k is left at month 6, not 125k. Only the Tranche 0 gate truly caps the loss.
- **Biggest blind spot:**
  - The CRO reviewer chose E (CFO): staging is called "stronger" while the downside needs about 731k of peak funding, and the SAR 230/seat price is not tested.
  - The CFO reviewer chose A (CSO): office coffee machines (OCS) are proposed with no machine cost or margin.
- **Missed by all:**
  - a zero-cost discovery step (about 20 buyer interviews and price quotes);
  - Saudization and delivery-vehicle licensing;
  - who owns the office fridges;
  - whether the SAR 230 price includes or excludes 15% VAT;
  - the actual cost per delivery drop at pilot volume;
  - a single agreed gate.

## Unified gate to Tranche 1 (chair's synthesis)
1. **Demand:** at least 5 offices on 3-month prepaid contracts, with at least 120 paid seats or at least 1,500 cups/month. Price at least SAR 220/seat or at least SAR 10/cup, ex-VAT. At least half of the offices must come from outside the CEO's network.
2. **Pilot pricing:** pilots charged at least 80% of list price.
3. **Licensing:** a written licensing route from a licensed professional, plus a licensable unit with a break clause.
4. **Operations:** at most 30 minutes per drop; waste at most 10%; at least 98% of drops at 5°C or below; CEO working at most 50 h/week.
5. **CEO:** confirms at least 24 months of personal runway outside the 300k, and a year-2 salary need of at most ~15k/month.
6. **Tranche 1 budget:** must include working capital explicitly.

## Kill / pivot criteria
- Fewer than 3 prepaid conversions, no licensable route, or a price below SAR 200/seat (SAR 9/cup): stop, or pivot to beans + brewer for offices.
- After launch: fewer than 8 offices at month 3, or fewer than 12 at month 6.
