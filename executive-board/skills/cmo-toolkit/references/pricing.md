# Pricing

- **Value-based pricing:**
  - **Ceiling:** the customer's economic value = the next-best alternative's price plus the value of differentiation.
  - **Floor:** unit cost plus required margin.
  - **Competitor reference points** shape perception.
- **Research:**
  - `van_westendorp` gives an acceptable price range (PMC–PME) and OPP/IPP. It is stated preference, a range, not willingness to pay.
  - `gabor_granger` gives the revenue- and profit-maximising price from purchase-likelihood curves. Haircut stated likelihoods (purchase intent typically overstates real purchase).
  - Conjoint analysis for feature/price trade-offs, when it's worth the cost.
  - Real tests (A/B or market tests) beat surveys.
- **Price-change maths:**
  - `price_change_breakeven`: with CM = 40% of price, a 10% price cut needs +33% volume to keep contribution flat. Cuts are usually worse than they look.
  - Subscriptions: `subscription_price_change` gives the break-even immediate loss and the break-even churn for steady state. Grandfathering existing customers lowers the risk.
- **Architecture:** good/better/best tiers, anchoring, bundles, annual prepay discounts (cash benefit to the CFO), and B2B volume tiers. Don't fight on price alone without a cost advantage.
- **Elasticity** (`elasticity`): estimate it from your own data (price tests, historical changes). Borrowed elasticities are weak evidence.
