# Market research and segmentation

- **Jobs to be done:** what progress the customer is trying to make, the current alternatives (including "do nothing"), and switching triggers and anxieties.
- **Segmentation:** use needs- and behaviour-based segments, not only demographics. Each segment needs a size, accessibility (channel), willingness to pay and a reason to choose us. Prioritise with a scored matrix (`decision_matrix`): attractiveness × ability to win.
- **Research design:**
  - Qualitative first: 12–20 interviews per segment are usually enough for theme saturation.
  - Then quantitative. For proportions, the margin of error is about ±1.96·√(p(1−p)/n); n ≈ 385 gives ±5% at p = 0.5. Avoid leading questions, and screen respondents properly.
- **Demand validation ladder** (weakest to strongest): survey interest → waitlist sign-ups → letters of intent → pre-orders or deposits → paid pilots → repeat purchase. Decisions involving large capital should require at least paid pilots.
- **Sources:** public statistics (for KSA, GASTAT), filings of listed peers, platform data (app stores, maps listings, social), and channel partner data. Cite retrieval dates.
