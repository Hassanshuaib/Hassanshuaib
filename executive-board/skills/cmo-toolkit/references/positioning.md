# Positioning, brand, competitive positioning

- **Positioning statement:** for [segment] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe], unlike [alternative].
- **Competitive map:** score competitors on the 2–3 attributes customers actually choose on (from research), not on attributes we like. Find white space that matters to a reachable segment.
- **Differentiation must be valued** (willingness to pay), **defensible** (hard to copy) and **communicable** (fits in one sentence).
- **Brand:** consistency across touchpoints, distinctive assets, and reviews/reputation (critical for local businesses: Google Maps and app-store ratings).
- **Competitive response:** anticipate how incumbents react (price cuts, imitation, channel lock-up) and pressure-test with the CSO. For full competitor deep-dives, use `board-finance-pack:competitive-analysis` if it is installed.
