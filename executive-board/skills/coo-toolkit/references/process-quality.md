# Process optimisation, Lean, Six Sigma, quality

- **Value-stream mapping:** list each step with its process time, wait time, inventory and defect rate. **Process cycle efficiency** = value-added time / total lead time; below 10% is common, and the opportunity is in the waits.
- **Lean wastes (TIMWOODS):** transport, inventory, motion, waiting, over-production, over-processing, defects, skills. Remedies: 5S, standard work, SMED (setup reduction), pull/kanban, poka-yoke (error-proofing).
- **DMAIC** for problems with unknown causes. Define (problem statement, CTQ, baseline), Measure (data, MSA), Analyse (root cause: 5 Whys, fishbone, Pareto, regression), Improve (pilot), Control (SPC, SOPs).
- **Metrics:**
  - `oee` = availability × performance × quality. World-class is about 85%; typical is 40–60%.
  - `dpmo_sigma`: DPMO and sigma level (with the 1.5σ shift convention).
  - `control_limits`: I-MR or p-chart. React to special causes only, and don't tamper with common-cause variation.
  - `fmea`: RPN = S × O × D. Severity ≥ 9 is treated first regardless of RPN.
- **Cost reduction:** use zero-based review or a spend cube (category × supplier × business unit). Separate one-off savings from run-rate savings. Check that savings don't move cost elsewhere (quality, churn).
- **Food, health and safety contexts:** HACCP critical control points and cold-chain monitoring. In KSA, SFDA and municipal (Balady) licensing; verify the current requirements.
