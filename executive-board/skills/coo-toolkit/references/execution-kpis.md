# Execution planning and operational KPIs

- **Execution plan:** phases with gates (pilot → scale), owners, durations from three-point estimates (`pert_estimate`: mean = (O + 4M + P)/6), the critical path, dependencies (licences, hiring, equipment lead times) and a contingency of 10–20% on time and cost for new operations.
- **Readiness checklist:** permits and licences, site, equipment, suppliers under contract, staff hired and trained, SOPs, IT/POS, quality and safety controls, customer service, and contingency plans.
- **KPI tree:** link financial outcomes (margin, cash) to operational drivers (throughput, yield, utilisation, on-time delivery, cost per unit, waste %, first-time-right, inventory turns, lead time). Give each one an owner, a target, a threshold and a data source. Keep it to 6–10 KPIs.
- **Reviewing others' assumptions:** for each one, ask whether the operation can physically deliver it (capacity, time, people) and at what cost (opex/capex), and what the ramp is. Mark it Accept, Adjust (new value and reason) or Reject.
