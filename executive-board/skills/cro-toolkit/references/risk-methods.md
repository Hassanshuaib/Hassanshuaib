# Risk identification and assessment

- **Pre-mortem:** "It is 18 months later and this failed. Write the story." Produce 3–5 distinct failure stories, each with its causal chain and an early-warning signal.
- **Categories:** strategic, market/demand, financial (liquidity, leverage, FX, rates), operational (capacity, quality, supply, key person), regulatory/legal, reputational, technology/cyber, ESG/safety.
- **Register** (`risk_register`): likelihood 1–5 and impact 1–5. Score ≥ 15, or impact 5 with likelihood ≥ 3, is CRITICAL. Scores 8–14 are SIGNIFICANT. The rest is WATCH. Record the evidence, the mitigation, the owner and a trigger.
- **FMEA** (`fmea`) for process risks: RPN = S × O × D. Severity ≥ 9 is first regardless of RPN.
- **Bow-tie:** threats → preventive controls → event → recovery controls → consequences. Good for safety, food quality, data and cyber events.
- **Standards for structure:** ISO 31000 (risk management principles) and COSO ERM. Use them for vocabulary, not bureaucracy.
- **Biases to challenge:** optimism, the planning fallacy (use reference-class forecasting: how long and how much did similar projects really take?), confirmation bias, sunk cost, and groupthink.
