# Reference sources (cite edition and date; verify current data online)

## Textbooks and standards (for methodology)

- Brealey, Myers & Allen, *Principles of Corporate Finance*: NPV, capital structure, real options.
- Berk & DeMarzo, *Corporate Finance*.
- Koller, Goedhart & Wessels (McKinsey), *Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies*: ROIC, value drivers, DCF.
- Damodaran, *Investment Valuation* and *The Dark Side of Valuation* (young and private firms).
- Rosenbaum & Pearl, *Investment Banking*: comps, precedents, LBO and M&A mechanics.
- CFA Institute curriculum (Corporate Issuers, Equity Valuation, Financial Statement Analysis).
- IFRS Foundation standards (IFRS 9, 15, 16; IAS 36). In KSA, SOCPA has adopted IFRS.

## Data (always record the retrieval date)

- **Damodaran Online** (pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar): implied ERP, country risk premiums, industry betas, margins and multiples by sector and region.
- **Saudi Arabia:**
  - SAMA (rates, monetary data)
  - Ministry of Finance / NDMC (government sukuk yields)
  - GASTAT (statistics, inflation)
  - ZATCA (VAT, zakat and tax rules)
  - Tadawul / Saudi Exchange (listed-company filings for peers)
  - Ministry of Investment (MISA)
- **Global:** company filings (SEC EDGAR, exchange filings), central-bank yield data, IMF WEO (GDP and inflation for long-run growth).

## Rules

- Never quote a number from memory as current: say "approximately (as of <date>) — verify".
- Cite what you actually retrieved, with its URL.
- When sources conflict, show the range.
