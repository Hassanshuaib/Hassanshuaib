# Private equity, LBO, M&A, due diligence

## LBO (`lbo`, and the `board-finance-pack:lbo-model` skill for Excel templates)

- **Returns drivers:** EBITDA growth, multiple expansion, and deleveraging (debt paydown plus cash build), less fees. `lbo` returns this **value-creation bridge**, and it reconciles exactly.
- **Debt capacity:** typically 3–6× EBITDA depending on stability. Check interest cover and FCF/debt each year, and test an exit at entry multiple and at −1×/−2× turns.
- **Target returns:** many funds target a gross IRR of about 20–25% and a MOIC of 2.0–3.0× over 4–6 years; cite a source if you use benchmarks. Returns from multiple expansion alone are speculative.

## M&A

- **Accretion/dilution** (`accretion_dilution`, and `board-finance-pack:merger-model`): EPS accretion is not value creation. The real test is `NPV = value of target standalone + synergies − price paid − integration costs > 0`.
- **Synergies:** separate cost from revenue synergies, and phase them in over 2–3 years. Revenue synergies get haircuts of about 50%+. Report the **break-even synergies** (`accretion_dilution`) and compare them with credible estimates.
- **Control premium and precedents:** see `valuation.md`. Paying the premium needs synergies at least as large as the premium.
- **Structure:** cash versus stock (who bears the risk), earn-outs for valuation gaps, and locked-box versus completion accounts.

## Due diligence (`board-finance-pack:dd-checklist` for full checklists)

- **Financial:** quality of earnings (normalised EBITDA, one-offs, owner costs), NWC peg (the normalised level), net debt and debt-like items, capex backlog, customer concentration, revenue recognition.
- **Tax:** zakat and corporate income tax exposure, VAT compliance (KSA VAT is 15%), transfer pricing.
- **Commercial, operational and legal:** coordinate with the COO, CMO, CSO and CRO. Red flags are summarised by severity.
- **IC memo:** thesis, valuation range, returns, key risks and mitigants, conditions, the 100-day plan.
