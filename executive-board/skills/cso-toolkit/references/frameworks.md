# Strategic frameworks: use when they change the answer

- **Five Forces:** rivalry, entry barriers, buyer power, supplier power, substitutes. The output is the **structural profitability** of the industry and its drivers. Concentration: `hhi` (US DOJ 2023 bands: > 1,800 is highly concentrated).
- **7 Powers (Helmer):** scale economies, network economies, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resource, process power. Each one needs a *benefit* and a *barrier*. A small business often has none at first. Say so honestly, and say which power it could build.
- **Value chain / activity system:** which activities create the cost or differentiation advantage, and do they fit together (Porter's fit)?
- **Ansoff:** market penetration < product development / market development < diversification, in rising order of risk. Diversification needs a specific capability rationale.
- **SWOT** only as a summary of analysis done elsewhere. **PESTEL** for entry into a new country or sector. In KSA, consider Vision 2030 programmes and regulation (licensing, Saudisation, foreign-ownership rules); verify the current rules.
- **Blue Ocean (eliminate, reduce, raise, create)** for crowded categories.
