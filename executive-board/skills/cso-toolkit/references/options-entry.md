# Market entry, business models, options and trade-offs

- **Entry modes:** organic, partnership/JV, licensing/franchise, acquisition. Compare them on control, speed, capital, risk and reversibility. For foreign markets, consider licensing and local-partner requirements.
- **Business-model canvas test:** who pays, for what, how often, at what gross margin; what the cost structure and the unit of scale are; what the cash cycle is (prepaid or credit).
- **Option comparison** (`decision_matrix`):
  1. Set the criteria (value, risk, capital, time-to-cash, fit, reversibility) and weights with the CEO.
  2. Score them with evidence.
  3. Check rank flips under ±20% weight changes. If the winner flips, the decision hinges on that criterion, so discuss it explicitly.
- **Scenario planning:** use 2 critical uncertainties to make a 2×2 of plausible futures. Find a strategy that is robust across them, or no-regret moves plus options.
- **Expected value** (`expected_value`): EV, spread and P(loss). Prefer staged commitments that buy information cheaply (real options).
- **Long-term plan:** a 3–5 year horizon, sequenced bets, resource allocation, and leading indicators per bet. Revisit it at every gate.
