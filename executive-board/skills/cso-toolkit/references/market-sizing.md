# Market sizing and adoption

- **Bottom-up** (`market_sizing`): segments × number of potential customers × purchase frequency × price.
  - **SAM** = the part you can serve with your model, geography and channels.
  - **SOM** = a realistic 3–5 year share, given competition, sales capacity and adoption speed.
  - Every number has a source or is labelled an estimate.
- **Top-down cross-check:** an industry report or official statistics × relevant share. Explain a gap above about 30% between bottom-up and top-down.
- **Adoption speed** (`bass_diffusion`):
  - p (innovation) is about 0.01–0.03 and q (imitation) about 0.3–0.5, as typical published ranges for consumer durables. Treat them as assumptions, and calibrate from analogues.
  - The shape matters for cash: slow early adoption deepens the cash trough.
- **Growth rates** (`cagr`): forecast market growth from drivers (population, penetration, price). Don't extrapolate a recent CAGR.
- **Share sanity check:** SOM share above about 10–20% of SAM within 3 years needs a specific reason (a new category, a structural advantage).
